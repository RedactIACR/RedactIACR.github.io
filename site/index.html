    How to play

    Every day, one paper from CRYPTO, EUROCRYPT or TCC is pulled from the Cryptology ePrint Archive and blacked out.

    • Type a word to reveal every occurrence of it in the paper.
    • Matching is case-insensitive and covers the word's other forms: require also uncovers requires, required and requiring. Related words with different meanings, like secure and security, stay separate.
    • Very common words and single letters are given to you for free.
    • Formulas are blacked out whole. Guess an identifier inside one — negl, poly, lambda — to reveal it. Pure notation like ℓ, k, n is never hidden.
    • You are reading the real paper, so figures, tables and the bibliography are all there. Use the zoom controls if the type is small.
    • You win when every word of the title is revealed.

    Your progress is saved in this browser. A new paper appears at 00:00 UTC.
